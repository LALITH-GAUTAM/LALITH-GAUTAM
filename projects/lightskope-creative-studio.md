# LightSkope Creative Studio Website

**Project type:** Business website  
**My role:** Co-founder and web developer  
**View:** https://lightskope.com/  
**Client work:** https://lightskope.com/portfolio

## Purpose

Present the studio's web, branding, content, and custom digital services, show real client work, and give business owners a way to explain a project.

## Work and technology

The site uses Next.js, React, and TypeScript. I built and maintain the studio website while collaborating with my co-founder on business and creative work.

The project includes a server-side quote endpoint that validates requests and forwards them to Formspree. Validation, provider acceptance, and email delivery are separate parts of the workflow.

## Scope

This is LightSkope's business website. It includes a portfolio section, but the site also represents the studio's services and inquiry process. Application source and operational configuration are maintained privately.
